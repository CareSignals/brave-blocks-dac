# Brave Blocks — Dependency Advocacy Center Review

This repository contains a separate, neutral Brave Blocks edition prepared for
Dependency Advocacy Center review. It keeps the playful emotional-learning
quests, Feeling Machine, spoken directions, Pause Portal, Safe Base,
meeting-preparation tools, and trusted-support pathways while removing
family-specific and belief-specific content.

## Prototype status

- This is an independent prototype for DAC review, not an official DAC service.
- It is DBT-informed emotional-learning play, not therapy, legal advice, a
  forensic interview, or a substitute for case-specific safety planning.
- Professional role and confidentiality wording should be approved by the
  relevant attorney, social worker, clinician, and organization before use.
- A child can point, use their own words, draw, or pass. The app never rewards
  disclosure, agreement, or a particular answer.
- Gameplay choices remain in the open session and are not saved or sent.
- No child account, analytics, case history, or cloud database is used.

## Child-facing quests

1. Vibe Mixer
2. Body Radar
3. Dragon Battle
4. Meeting Loadout
5. Talk Power-Up
6. Build Mode
7. Safety Power-Ups
8. Pixel Parkour
9. Music Power-Up
10. Courage Campfire
11. Feeling Machine

The Courage Campfire uses fictional character stories only. The Music Power-Up
opens a neutral music landing page only after a deliberate tap and does not
observe activity outside Brave Blocks.

## Adult companion

The gated Grown-up Guide links to the existing Power-Up Pals adult companion
for co-regulation context and caregiver/trusted-support pathways.
